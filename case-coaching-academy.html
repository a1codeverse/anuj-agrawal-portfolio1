PROJECT CONCEPT · EDUCATION

A coaching website that feels ready for real learners.

This independent concept explores how an Indian coaching institute can present Classes 6–12, board preparation, foundation learning and IIT-JEE / NEET programs through a clear, result-focused website.

The design direction uses familiar coaching-site patterns — course pathways, a visible admission enquiry, academic support, result highlights and frequently asked questions — while keeping its own branding and layout.

WHAT THE WEBSITE INCLUDES

Familiar to families.
Easy to navigate.

The homepage is designed around the questions students and parents tend to ask: which courses are available, what kind of support is offered, how progress is measured and how to enquire.

01Course pathways

Filterable cards for Classes 6–8 foundation, Classes 9–12 boards, IIT-JEE and NEET.

02Results & trust

A dedicated result snapshot area; figures are clearly marked for verification before public use.

03Student support

Sections for weekly tests, study modules, doubt clearing, hybrid learning and library facilities.

04Admission enquiries

Clear calls to action with a responsive enquiry form and validation feedback.

05Responsive design

Mobile navigation, scalable course cards and content designed to work on phone, tablet and desktop.

06Useful interactions

Course filters, accessible FAQ accordions, mobile menu and subtle scroll transitions.

Demo boundary: Sankalp Career Classes is a fictional demonstration identity. The tagline and result figures are drawn from the reference screenshot supplied for this concept, and all figures, facilities, courses, photos, contact details and claims must be confirmed and approved by the eventual institute before a production launch. This is not a completed client project.
