01 THE IDEA

A simple, Hindi-first school website direction.

मुखपृष्ठविद्यालय परिचयशैक्षणिक जानकारीप्रवेश प्रक्रियासूचना पटलगतिविधियाँसंपर्क

This concept uses Hindi-first messaging and a familiar, simple structure for schools serving families who prefer regional-language information. It can be adapted for a state-board school; specific board affiliation, school name and notices must be verified before a real site is published.

Designed for: Hindi-medium and regional schools.

Design priority: make the important information easy to find, guide visitors to the appropriate next step, and keep the experience consistent across phone, tablet and desktop layouts.

02 PROPOSED FEATURE AREAS

Built around real
visitor needs.

The cards describe the proposed design direction. A feature is only considered working after it has been implemented and tested.

01

Hindi-first information

Important labels and key messages are written in Hindi for quick understanding.

02

Admission guidance

A simple route to admission details, required documents and enquiry contact.

03

Notice & dates board

A visible area for school notices, holidays, meeting dates and examination schedules.

04

Academic information

Space for classes, school timings, subjects and curriculum details.

05

Optional language switch

A bilingual setup can be added when the school needs Hindi and English content.

06

Easy contact options

Phone, location, email or WhatsApp contact can be shown according to school preference.

03 SCOPE & NEXT STEPS

From concept to
a working website.

The exact deliverables, integrations, costs and timeline would be agreed after understanding the project requirements.

What this concept covers

  • Hindi-first visual layout and sample content
  • A clear notice and information hierarchy
  • Responsive layout for parents using phones
  • Optional English-language counterpart as a separate scope
  • Fictional sample school name and content

What a live implementation may need

Live publication needs school-approved Hindi copy, verified address and affiliation information, a privacy-appropriate enquiry process, and an agreed way for staff to update notices. A Hindi/English language switch also requires the translated content to be prepared.
