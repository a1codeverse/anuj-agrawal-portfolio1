01 THE IDEA

A modern website concept for an Indian CBSE-style school.

HomeAbout the SchoolAcademicsAdmissionsFacilitiesGalleryNoticesContact

This concept is designed around what an Indian private-school website visitor commonly needs first: a clear school introduction, admission information, academic details, important notices and an easy way to contact the institution. The sample school name and information are fictional and are used for demonstration only.

Designed for: CBSE / ICSE-style private schools.

Design priority: make the important information easy to find, guide visitors to the appropriate next step, and keep the experience consistent across phone, tablet and desktop layouts.

03 SCOPE & NEXT STEPS

From concept to
a working website.

The exact deliverables, integrations, costs and timeline would be agreed after understanding the project requirements.

What this concept covers

  • Responsive homepage and section layout
  • Academics, facilities, notice and gallery page concepts
  • Admission enquiry CTA and contact layout
  • Sample content clearly labelled as fictional
  • Mobile-friendly content hierarchy

What a live implementation may need

A real school deployment may also need an enquiry submission endpoint, content management, approved school photography, verified affiliation details, privacy notices and staff workflows. Student records, payments or parent login would be a separate secure implementation.
