01 THE IDEA

A polished campus-led website direction for an Indian private school.

HomeAboutAcademicsCampus & FacilitiesSports & ActivitiesAchievementsAdmissionsContact

This concept uses richer campus-style visuals and a considered content hierarchy for established Indian private schools. It is intended to demonstrate a premium aesthetic, while keeping practical parent tasks—such as finding admissions information and contacting the school—easy to spot.

Designed for: Established private schools and larger campuses.

Design priority: make the important information easy to find, guide visitors to the appropriate next step, and keep the experience consistent across phone, tablet and desktop layouts.

03 SCOPE & NEXT STEPS

From concept to
a working website.

The exact deliverables, integrations, costs and timeline would be agreed after understanding the project requirements.

What this concept covers

  • Premium homepage and campus-led visual style
  • Academics, facilities, life-at-school and admissions sections
  • Mobile-friendly navigation and clear calls to action
  • Prospectus download area as a design concept
  • Fictional sample branding and imagery style

What a live implementation may need

Before launch, the school must supply licensed photography, verified academic and affiliation details, approved achievements, current admission dates and an agreed method for staff to maintain content. Any download, enquiry or payment workflow must be connected and tested.
