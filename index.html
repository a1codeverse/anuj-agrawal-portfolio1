09 COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things
you might be wondering.

Still have a question? Ask the portfolio assistant or reach out directly.

What types of websites do you work on?+

I focus on websites for schools, coaching institutes and local businesses, along with selected storefront and dashboard concepts. The final scope depends on your requirements.

How much does a website cost?+

Cost depends on the pages, functionality, integrations and content needed. Share a few details through the enquiry form so we can discuss a project-specific estimate.

How long will a website take?+

The timeline depends on project scope, required functionality and how quickly content and feedback are available. A realistic timeline is discussed after reviewing requirements.

Will the website work on mobile phones?+

Responsive design is part of the planned approach. Each final implementation should be tested at suitable phone, tablet and desktop sizes before launch.

Can I ask for features like admissions or student login?+

We can discuss those features. Admission enquiry forms are different from a full student login system; secure accounts, databases, notifications and other advanced features require appropriate implementation and scope.

How do I get started?+

Use the project enquiry form or start a WhatsApp conversation. You can begin with your goal even if you are not sure about the technical details yet.
