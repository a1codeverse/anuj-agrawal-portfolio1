01 THE IDEA

A useful online home for a local business.

HomeAbout the BusinessServicesService DetailFAQsContact

A local business site should answer simple questions quickly: What does the business do? Who does it help? Where does it operate? How can someone contact it? This concept uses that journey as the centre of the page.

Designed for: Local services, shops and small businesses.

Design priority: make the important information easy to find, guide visitors to the appropriate next step, and keep the experience consistent across phone, tablet and desktop layouts.

03 SCOPE & NEXT STEPS

From concept to
a working website.

The exact deliverables, integrations, costs and timeline would be agreed after understanding the project requirements.

What this concept covers

  • Business homepage and service section concept
  • About, service detail and contact layout
  • WhatsApp / email contact action
  • Sample branding and illustrative content
  • Mobile layout and clear enquiry CTA

What a live implementation may need

A live project needs the real business details, accurate service descriptions, licensed photos, location and hours if applicable. Online booking, payment, CRM or a server-backed form would need separately agreed integration.
