01 THE IDEA

A storefront demo focused on product discovery.

StorefrontCategoriesProduct DetailCart ConceptFAQ / Shipping PolicyContact

This demo concentrates on the front-end shopping experience: how product categories, product cards, details and next steps can be organised. It does not claim to process payments or run a real online shop.

Designed for: Small brands and businesses exploring online sales.

Design priority: make the important information easy to find, guide visitors to the appropriate next step, and keep the experience consistent across phone, tablet and desktop layouts.

03 SCOPE & NEXT STEPS

From concept to
a working website.

The exact deliverables, integrations, costs and timeline would be agreed after understanding the project requirements.

What this concept covers

  • Visual storefront / product-grid demonstration
  • Category and product detail design direction
  • Responsive layout concept
  • Sample products and pricing clearly marked as illustrative if shown
  • No live payment or order-processing claim

What a live implementation may need

A production shop needs a backend/catalog, order and inventory handling, secure payment-provider integration, shipping/tax rules, privacy and refund policies, email notifications and end-to-end testing. These are not included in a static visual demo.
